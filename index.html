About

I turn complexity into systems that work.

I work at the intersection of data, operations, and technology. I turn fragmented processes and ambiguous problems into measurable and scalable solutions.

The short version
I am interested in what happens between a problem being identified and a solution actually becoming part of the process.

My work is about finding that gap, understanding it, simplifying it, and building something better.

How I work

01

Question

I start with why. Before building anything, I want to understand the problem behind the problem.

02

Diagnose

I break complex processes into smaller parts and identify where information, time or decisions get lost.

03

Build

I use data, automation and technology to turn the insight into something people can actually use.

04

Scale

I look beyond the solution once it is in place. I think about how it can be adopted, made repeatable and scaled to create measurable impact.

How I think
“The best solution is rarely the most complicated one. It is the one that makes the underlying problem easier to understand.”

I enjoy working on problems where the answers are not immediately obvious. Data may be fragmented, processes may have evolved over time, and different teams may see the same problem differently. For me, the challenge is finding the clarity within that complexity.
